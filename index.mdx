The Supercharged Citation Picker brings blazingly fast, system-wide academic citation insertion to every text field on macOS. Powered by Alfred, it reads your `.bib` file directly and lets you search, insert, and manage BibTeX references without switching apps — supporting Pandoc, LaTeX, Multi-Markdown, org-mode, iA Writer, and more.

## Key features at a glance

- **System-wide** — works in every text field of every app, no integration required
- **Blazingly fast** — reloads a 4,000-entry library in under 200 milliseconds
- **10+ citation formats** — Pandoc, LaTeX, Multi-Markdown, org-mode, eta template, iA Writer, wikilink, tag, bare citekey, and more
- **Dual library** — pick from two `.bib` files simultaneously
- **Reference management** — add entries from DOI/ISBN, auto-file PDFs, delete entries

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the workflow">
    Grab the latest `.alfredworkflow` release from [GitHub](https://github.com/chrisgrieser/alfred-bibtex-citation-picker/releases/latest) and double-click to install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your library path">
    Open Alfred Preferences → Workflows → Supercharged Citation Picker → Configure Workflow and enter the path to your `.bib` file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a citation format">
    Type `scp` in Alfred, select **Change citation format**, and pick your preferred style (default: Pandoc).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start citing">
    Press your hotkey or type `ct` in Alfred from any text field, search for a reference, and press <kbd>↵</kbd> to insert it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
